# Yogas & Doshas

BPHS and Phaladeepika list hundreds of yogas. A yoga only gives results
**if the planets forming it are strong** (good dignity in D1 and D9, not
combust, not in 6/8/12 unless it is a Viparita yoga), and mainly **during
the dashas or antardashas of those planets**. Grade each one: strong,
moderate or nominal.

## Check manually (not auto-detected)

- **Lakshmi Yoga:** the 9th lord strong in a kendra or trikona in own or
  exaltation sign, and the Lagna lord strong. Brings wealth and grace.
- **Saraswati Yoga:** Jupiter, Venus and Mercury in kendras, trikonas or the
  2nd, with Jupiter strong. Brings learning and the arts.
- **Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga:** the 9th and 10th lords associated. A
  premier raja yoga for career and dharma.
- **Shakata Yoga:** the Moon in the 6th, 8th or 12th from Jupiter.
  Fluctuating fortune. Cancelled if the Moon is in a kendra from the Lagna.
- **Daridra Yoga:** the 11th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th. Monetary
  struggle (check for mitigations).
- **Papa/Shubha Kartari:** malefics or benefics hemming the Lagna, Moon or a house.
- **Chandal (Guru-Chandala):** Jupiter + Rahu, reported by the script.
  Unorthodox beliefs or challenges with teachers. It can also produce
  rebels and innovators. Judge Jupiter's strength.
- **Grahana Yoga:** the Sun or Moon with Rahu or Ketu (reported). Emotional
  or ego eclipses, sometimes spiritual depth. Worse within ~10° and
  near eclipse births.
- **Vargottama Lagna or Moon:** a strong, stable personality.
- **Pravrajya (renunciation):** four or more planets in one sign including
  the 10th lord. Rare. Signals spirituality.
- **Jaimini Raja Yoga:** the Atmakaraka and Amatyakaraka associated, or
  the lords of the AL and 7th from the AL associated.
- **Karakamsa:** the sign of the Atmakaraka in the D9. Planets in and
  aspecting it show the soul's path (Ketu: moksha; Jupiter: teaching or
  scripture; Venus: arts or government; and so on).

## Doshas — honest, balanced presentation

### Mangal (Kuja / Manglik) Dosha
- **Formation:** Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from the Lagna
  (the most important), the Moon, or Venus. By that definition about 40–60%
  of people qualify, so say so to defuse fear.
- **Classical cancellations** (any one reduces or cancels it): Mars in its
  own sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn). Mars in Leo or
  Aquarius per some texts. Mars conjunct or aspected by Jupiter. Mars in
  the 2nd in Gemini or Virgo, the 4th in Aries or Scorpio, the 7th in
  Cancer or Capricorn, the 8th in Sagittarius or Pisces, or the 12th in
  Taurus or Libra. Both partners Manglik (it cancels out). The dosha is
  considered to weaken after age 28.
- **Real meaning:** a dynamic, assertive temperament that needs a partner
  who matches its energy, or conscious anger management. It is not a curse.

### Kala Sarpa Yoga
- All seven planets on one side of the Rahu–Ketu axis. This is **not in
  BPHS**. It is a later, popular concept that many respected astrologers
  (including B.V. Raman) treat with scepticism. If a planet is conjunct
  a node but outside the axis arc, it is "partial".
- Typical reading: a life of intense focus, delays followed by sudden
  rise, karmic intensity. Many accomplished people have it.

### Pitra Dosha
- The Sun or the 9th house/lord afflicted by Rahu, Ketu or Saturn. Read
  as a call to honour ancestors (shraddha, tarpana, charity). Never
  frame it as a curse.
