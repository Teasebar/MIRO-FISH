# Upayas (Remedies)

Principle: remedies **strengthen weak functional benefics** and **pacify
afflicting malefics**. Order them from free to costly and from inner to
outer. Character and conduct come first: the classical texts say right
conduct (sadachara) is the greatest remedy.

Also useful: the **Navagraha stotra** (Vyasa) for general balance, the
**Maha Mrityunjaya mantra** for health anxieties, the **Gayatri mantra**
for clarity, and fasting on the planet's day (Saturday for Saturn,
Thursday for Jupiter, and so on), if health permits.

## Gemstone rules (important)

- Recommend a gem **only for functional benefics**, usually the Lagna
  lord, the 5th lord, the 9th lord, or a yogakaraka. Never for the lord
  of the 6th, 8th or 12th, or for a functional malefic: a gem amplifies
  the planet, it does not "fix" it.
- Never for a planet that is debilitated and afflicted without careful
  analysis.
- Avoid enemy combinations together (for example ruby with blue sapphire,
  or pearl with hessonite).
- Blue sapphire and hessonite are potent. Classical practice suggests a
  trial period of a few days.
- Mention the cost-free alternatives and that a gem is optional. Suggest
  consulting a trusted local astrologer and jeweller for weight and
  quality. Never push purchases.

## Time-bound remedies
- **Sade Sati / Saturn transit:** Saturday Hanuman Chalisa, Shani mantra,
  oil daana, serving elders and labourers, discipline. Avoid shortcuts
  and dishonesty.
- **Difficult dasha:** the dasha lord's mantra and charity on its day,
  worship of its deity, and living out the planet's positive qualities.
- **Mangal dosha:** Hanuman worship on Tuesday, Mangal Chandika stotra.
  Kumbh vivah is a traditional custom. Present it neutrally and never
  insist on it.

## Framing
- Keep remedies practical and doable. Three well-chosen remedies beat a
  long list.
- Tie each remedy to a reason in the chart ("Saturn, your yogakaraka, is
  weak in the 8th, so strengthening it through disciplined service...").
- Respect the user's faith: offer secular equivalents (a meditation
  routine, volunteering, journaling on the planet's themes) for users
  who prefer non-religious practice.
