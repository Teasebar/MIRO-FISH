*Bhavat bhavam*: the house as far from a house as it is from the Lagna
repeats its theme (the 9th from the 9th is the 5th, so the 5th also speaks
of grandfather and fortune).

## 4. Functional nature by Lagna (Parashari)

General rules: lords of trikonas (5, 9) are always auspicious. Lords of 3, 6
and 11 are inauspicious. The 8th lord is generally inauspicious (except Sun
and Moon, which own only one sign and so carry no 8th-lordship blemish).
Natural benefics owning kendras lose their beneficence (*kendradhipati
dosha*). A planet ruling both a kendra and a trikona is a **yogakaraka**.
Lords of 2 and 7 are marakas.

through hard work. These are general guidelines. Always weigh the actual
placement and dignity.

## 5. Strength & condition rules

- **Dignity ladder** (strongest → weakest): exalted > moolatrikona > own >
  great friend > friend > neutral > enemy > great enemy > debilitated.
- **Vargottama**: same sign in D1 and D9, which is a big boost (counts like
  own-sign strength).
- **D9 check**: always cross-check a planet's D9 dignity. A planet exalted
  in D1 but debilitated in D9 promises much and delivers less, and the
  reverse is also true.
- **Combustion** (asta): the planet's significations are suppressed or
  absorbed by the ego (Sun). It is stronger in effect for Venus and Mercury
  in personal matters. Mercury is often combust and suffers less from it.
- **Retrograde** (vakri): Cheshta bala makes it *strong*, but it acts in an
  introspective or delayed way and repeats themes. A retrograde planet that
  is exalted can behave as if debilitated, and the reverse (an opinion held
  by some authorities).
- **Graha yuddha** (planetary war): two of Mars–Saturn within 1° in
  longitude. The planet with lower longitude (or the one that is northern,
  by latitude) generally wins. The loser is weakened.
- **Digbala** (directional strength): Jupiter and Mercury in the 1st,
  Moon and Venus in the 4th, Saturn in the 7th, Sun and Mars in the 10th.
- **Gandanta**: the last 3°20' of the water signs (Cn, Sc, Pi) and the
  first 3°20' of the fire signs (Le, Sg, Ar) are karmic knots. A Moon or
  Lagna here needs careful handling.
- **Mrityu bhaga / avastha** are optional refinements. Mention them only if
  the user is advanced.
- **Ashtakavarga**: a planet's sign with SAV ≥ 28 supports good results,
  ≤ 24 is weak. A planet's own BAV of ≥ 5 bindus in its sign is strong.
  The 11th house SAV should exceed the 12th (more income than expense), and
  houses 1, 10 and 11 should be strong.
- **Hemming** (papakartari): malefics on both sides of a house or planet
  squeeze it. Benefics on both sides (shubhakartari) protect it.

## 7. House-lord placement principles

- The lord of house X placed in house Y joins the significations of both
  ("the 10th lord in the 5th: career through intellect, creativity,
  teaching or advisory roles").
- Lords of kendras and trikonas placed in kendras and trikonas give strong
  results. A lord placed in 6/8/12 weakens its house, unless it is itself
  a dusthana lord (Viparita Raja Yoga).
- A lord in its own house is strong. A lord aspecting its own house protects it.
- The lord of the 6th, 8th or 12th associated with a house troubles that house.
- A house is judged by its lord's strength, its occupants, the aspects it
  receives, its SAV bindus, its karaka, and the same house from the Moon
  and in the relevant varga.
- **Aspects** (graha drishti, whole sign): all planets aspect the 7th from
  themselves. Mars also aspects the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th,
  Saturn the 3rd and 10th. Rahu and Ketu take the 5th and 9th per some
  authorities (the script lists them, so note the debate). Jupiter's
  aspect protects, Saturn's restricts or delays, Mars's energises or
  conflicts.
